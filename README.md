# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping behavior to understand **customer spending patterns, product performance, discount usage, subscription behavior, customer loyalty, shipping preferences, and revenue contribution**.

The project follows an end-to-end **Data Analyst workflow**, starting with raw customer shopping data, cleaning and transforming the data using Python, answering business questions using PostgreSQL, and finally creating an interactive dashboard in Power BI.

### End-to-End Workflow

**Raw Data → Python/Pandas → Data Cleaning → PostgreSQL → SQL Business Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze customer shopping behavior and answer important business questions that can help a retail business understand its customers and make data-driven decisions.

The analysis focuses on:

* Customer revenue and spending
* Customer subscription behavior
* Discount effectiveness
* Product performance
* Customer loyalty
* Repeat purchasing behavior
* Shipping preferences
* Age-group revenue contribution

# 📊 Business Questions

### 1. Revenue by Gender

**What is the total revenue generated by male vs. female customers?**

This analysis compares total revenue generated by male and female customers.

---

### 2. Discount Users with Above-Average Spending

**Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?**

This identifies customers who used discounts while making purchases above the overall average purchase amount.

---

### 3. Top 5 Products by Average Rating

**Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review rating?**

This identifies the products receiving the highest average customer ratings.

---

### 4. Subscriber vs. Non-Subscriber Spending

**Do subscribed customers spend more?**

The analysis compares:

* Average purchase amount
* Total revenue

between:

* Subscribers
* Non-subscribers

---

### 5. Standard vs. Express Shipping

**What is the difference in average purchase amount between Standard and Express Shipping customers?**

This analysis compares purchasing value based on the selected shipping method.

---

### 6. Products with Highest Discount Purchase Percentage

**Which 5 products have the highest percentage of purchases with discounts applied?**

This identifies products where discounts are most frequently associated with purchases.

---

### 7. Customer Segmentation

Customers are segmented into three groups based on their number of previous purchases:

| Segment   | Previous Purchases |
| --------- | -----------------: |
| New       |                0–1 |
| Returning |                2–5 |
| Loyal     |        More than 5 |

The analysis shows the number of customers in each segment.

---

### 8. Top 3 Products Within Each Category

**What are the top 3 most purchased products within each category?**

A SQL window function is used to rank products within each category and identify the top 3 products.

---

### 9. Repeat Buyers vs. Subscription

**Are customers who are repeat buyers — more than 5 previous purchases — also likely to subscribe?**

The analysis compares subscription behavior among customers who have made more than 5 previous purchases.

---

### 10. Revenue Contribution by Age Group

**What is the revenue contribution of each age group?**

Customers are grouped by age range and their contribution to total revenue is analyzed.
